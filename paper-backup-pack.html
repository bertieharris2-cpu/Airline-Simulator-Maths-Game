✈ AIRLINE SIMULATOR — PAPER BACKUP PACK

One round of the airline game on paper. Use it when the laptop or whiteboard won't play.

How to run a paper round

  1. Briefing. Read the round card aloud. One decision at a time.
  2. Decision cards. He circles a choice. Talk about the numbers first, then decide.
  3. The big calculation. One sheet per round. You scaffold the method; the sheet gives the numbers and space.
  4. The flight plays out. Fill in the round record together: seats sold, ticket money, costs, profit, cash.
  5. Why did that happen? Say it in one or two lines. Fill in a review card.
  6. Problem event (optional) and Captain's challenge (optional, every second round).
