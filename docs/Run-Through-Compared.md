# Fable's run-through compared with the game

*Fable's document, "A Run-Through to Week 3, Sum by Sum", set against what the game actually asks. The game's figures come from a scripted run of the prototype (`src/opening/tests/runthrough.test.js`, 6 Oct 2026). The full log is in [Run-Through-In-Game.md](Run-Through-In-Game.md).*

## The short answer

Fable describes 40 sums, most of them "required by hand", over the first 15 days. It works from workbook v2: a game that starts on 1 September, with a rival, a storm choice, played weekends, a second aircraft in Week 2 and a jet in Week 3. The game is not built that way.

**The game's 29 days to the end of Week 3** are Launch Day, Days 1–4, the weekend and Weeks 1–3. In that time the pupil:

| What the pupil does | How many |
| --- | --- |
| **Types** a figure | 7 |
| **Builds** a sum (chooses the two figures and the sign; the model does the arithmetic) | 2 |
| **Chooses** an answer from three | 8 |

Everything else is worked out and shown by the model.

**Matches with Fable:**
- The Launch Day figures are the same to the pound.
- The fuel order of £650 matches.
- The litres for the mixed day (420 L) match.

**Not in the game at all:**
- column addition of costs;
- litres worked out by the pupil;
- service-length sums;
- the duty-day sum;
- the week as seven days added up;
- anything in Week 2 or Week 3 of Fable's story: second aircraft, finance, lease, Amsterdam, Madrid, the E190, seasons, time zones;
- the Captain's challenges.

## C. Fable's own arithmetic

**All of Fable's sums are arithmetically correct**, and its costs follow the same rules as the game. Checked: 615; 1,830; 2,410; 3,371 (with the second crew); 468; 650; 11,978; 7,000; 5,600; 6,120; 127,200p; 14,450; 3,213; 34,000.

Three cautions:
- **Sum 31** is right (40 × 1.4 = 56), but its explanation ("ten per cent is 4, four tens are 16") is muddled. "40 × 0.4 = 16, and 40 + 16 = 56" is clearer.
- **Sums 35 and 37** use the E190 at 900 km/h, but the workbook says 800 km/h. Fable flags this itself.
- **Sum 15** uses a Dublin fare of £90. The workbook's Dublin base fare is £80, and that is what the game uses.

## D. For workbook v3 and Balance Report v3

**What the game uses now, so v3 lines up:**
- **Calendar:** the game starts on **Sunday 12 May 2030** (Launch Day), Day 1 is Monday 13 May, and Week 1 is 20–26 May.
  - The workbook's start date (1 September 2030) and its Calendar, Market, Events, Challenges and Mechanics sheets are **not read** by the game.
  - Each story beat, its fuel price and its news live in the game's own data (`data-world`).
  - If v3 should drive the calendar, that is a change to the game, not just the data.
- **Order of ideas:** Launch Day ticket revenue and profit; Day 1 snacks; Day 2 fuel; Day 3 times of day and crews; Day 4 the second route; Week 1 the weekly projection. The workbook's Mechanics sheet has fuel on day 3, a rival on day 4 and a second aircraft on day 8. The Calendar puts snacks on day 12.
- **Sheets the game reads:** Settings, Archetypes, Routes (Dublin and Paris only), Aircraft (Twin Otter, Saab 340, ATR 72) and Airports.
- **Fuel prices in the game:** £1.20 on Launch Day and Day 1 (free), £1.30 on Day 2, £1.40 on Days 3–4, the weekend and Week 1, £1.45 in Weeks 2–3, then £1.50 in June.
- **Fares:** Dublin starts at £80, Paris at £90, both in £10 steps from £70 to £110.

**Balance:**
- **"Time to afford" is 0 months for every player.** The Saab at £9,000 and the ATR at £15,000 are both affordable long before the projection is made (cash on 1 September is about £107,000). v3 needs aircraft prices, or a different emergency money (now £20,000), that make waiting a real decision.
- **The Year 1 projection falls short by about a quarter,** because demand keeps growing into the autumn.
- **Legs not on a quarter hour** (Saab and ATR at 450 km/h on Paris, Amsterdam and Geneva) give pence in costs.

**Decisions for you before v3 is wired in.** Fable's run-through assumes all of these; the game has none of them.
1. **Second aircraft:** in Week 2 (with finance and lease), or at the Year 1 review as now?
2. **Rival:** should Red Kite's fares and the events (storm choice, crew sickness) come into the opening?
3. **Weekends:** played with weekend demand, or run by themselves as now?
4. **Captain's challenges:** should they be scheduled? They are the only place long multiplication and short division would appear as bare sums.
5. **£5 fare steps:** Fable suggests them on one route in Week 2, to bring in long multiplication.
6. **More sums by hand:** the game asks for one new figure a day, then hands it to the model. Do you want some figures (e.g. the day's costs, litres, the week as an addition) asked by hand as well, as Fable assumes?
