## Part 4, point by point

**1. The review's total, difference and mean are Build steps, with your feedback: confirmed.**
- **Sunday review.** The seven daily figures are chips. The pupil taps figures and operations into the bar, then Checks. The game fills in the answer with a short count-up and stamps it Checked. Errors get a Query stamp with your plain hints:
  - "Wed 4's result isn't in the total yet";
  - "Each day should be counted once";
  - "We're finding the total for the week: which operation joins amounts together?".
- **Projected against actual.** Two chips (Projected, Actual) and an operation. The larger minus the smaller is correct, and the result reads "£1,036 less than planned", in red when below plan.
- **Week and month reviews from day 29.** The same cards:
  - the difference between the last two weeks;
  - three weeks added, then ÷ 3;
  - the average profit a day for each week.
- **Monthly reports.** From 2 December they ask for the average week (month total ÷ the number of weeks, counted as Mondays in the month: 4 or 5).
- **Chapter accounts.** These are Build too: total revenue and total costs from the months, profit as revenue − costs, then planned against what happened.
- **Teacher override.** The teacher panel has a checkbox to set Calculate (typed totals) for a pupil who needs the practice.
