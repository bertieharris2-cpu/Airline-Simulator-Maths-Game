Every option has the same content: header, navigation, KPI cards, route map, fleet, route performance, finance chart, fuel and operations, alerts, and one big Start the day button. Only the look, density and emphasis change. Open each at full screen on the laptop, then on the whiteboard.

Which is strongest?

Mid-blue (option 1) is the best fit for the final game. It keeps the "real airline" excitement of the dark references, but is easier on the eye for a whole lesson and reads well on an interactive whiteboard, where very dark pages lose detail and very pale pages wash out. Amber is saved for money and the main action, so the eye goes to the decision first.

Worth borrowing from the others: the welcome hero with today's decision (light and airy), the route-expansion opportunity cards (dark premium), and the departures board plus season goals (operations command).
